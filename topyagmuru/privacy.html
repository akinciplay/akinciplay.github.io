Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · This policy covers the game Brick Rain (called Top Yağmuru in Turkish).

1. Who we are

Volkan Akıncı (Akıncı Play), Türkiye. Contact: akinciplay@gmail.com. This notice is provided under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish data protection law (KVKK).

2. Data we collect

Stays on your device: your level progress, stars, gold, ball skins, settings and statistics are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us.
Leaderboard (optional): if you join the Endless Mode leaderboard, the player name you choose, your best score, the score date and a randomly generated player ID are stored on our server. Your name and score are visible to all players. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name.
Advertising: our ad partner Google AdMob processes information such as device model, operating system, app version, approximate location (from IP address) and, if you allow it, your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
Purchases: product, date and transaction details are handled by the store (Apple App Store / Google Play). Your card details never reach us.

We do not collect: contacts, photos, microphone, precise location.

3. Why we use it

To run the game; to provide the leaderboard; to deliver purchases; to show ads; to prevent cheating and abuse.

4. Legal bases

Performance of a contract (providing the game and leaderboard), legitimate interests (security, preventing cheating), legal obligation (purchase records) and consent (personalised ads).

5. Who we share it with

Data is shared with the providers we use to run the service: Google Firebase (leaderboard server, located in the EU, Belgium), Google AdMob (ads), and Apple and Google (stores and payments). These providers may process data outside your country under appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your data.

Ad consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for ad consent when it starts; on iPhone, Apple's tracking permission is also requested. If you decline, the game works exactly the same, only ads are not personalised. You can change your choice in the game under Me › Ad Privacy Choices or in your phone settings (iOS: Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Privacy › Ads).

6. How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries: while the game is live or until you ask us to delete them; deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. To exercise them, including deleting your leaderboard entry, email akinciplay@gmail.com with your in-game name; we reply free of charge within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you notice that a child under 13 has entered a name on the leaderboard, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections; each player can only write their own leaderboard entry.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be announced in the game.
